MyNamaz Privacy Policy

2026-10-15.2 · MyNamaz · tastaq

Who is responsible

MyNamaz is a religious app helping Muslims with prayer times and the Qibla direction. Only religious announcements are accepted; commercial announcements must relate to Islam and pass owner review. Voluntary developer support provides no paid advantages. The responsible developer is an individual in Kazakhstan, named tastaq on Google Play. For privacy, correction and deletion requests: @azamatdos, https://t.me/azamatdos. This policy does not claim a registered company or legal entity exists. MyNamaz has no ordinary user accounts or developer-operated user-data server.

Location and prayer times

With your operating-system permission, the app obtains a current location for country, timezone, prayer times and Qibla. It does not continuously track routes in the background. Coordinates and place names are cached on the phone. Reverse geocoding sends precise coordinates and the selected language to the system service: Apple on iOS and the available Android provider on Android. This is external processing, not solely local calculation. In Kazakhstan, requests to api.muftyat.kz contain the selected public settlement coordinates and schedule year, not your GPS coordinates directly. The nearest settlement is selected locally. Elsewhere, times are calculated on the device after country-specific consent and are not an official timetable. Timezone and Qibla calculations are local. You can disable location in OS settings; location-dependent functions may become unavailable.

Maps, Telegram and network information

The map requests tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org; tile numbers reveal the displayed map area. Announcements are requested from https://t.me/s/namaz_help_kz; individual posts are verified through t.me. Images load from addresses in the public feed, including its CDN. Providers receive your IP address, request time and URL, and technical headers. GPS is not appended to feed or image requests. These services may keep logs under their own policies; MyNamaz does not set their retention or promise that logs do not exist. Maps, feeds and images involve their respective network requests; ordinary cached announcements may display offline. Commercial announcements exist, but this version has no advertising SDK, advertising identifier, analytics SDK or developer-operated crash upload. The OS and stores may separately process diagnostics under their own settings.

Sensors, camera, notifications and settings

Compass, orientation and motion are processed on the phone for Qibla and the panorama; our code does not send sensor values to external services. The panorama “camera” models a virtual view direction: this version does not request hardware camera access or record video or audio. Editor photos are selected through the system file/photo picker on your action, without reading the whole gallery. The address book is not accessed: you enter request contacts. Language, appearance, announcement languages, consent and reminder settings are local. Reminders are scheduled locally with permission; their calculation location, contents and dates are stored. Disabling or rescheduling cancels the relevant pending reminders; these are not server push messages.

Requests, contacts, photos and reports

A request draft stores selected languages and texts, category, help type, number of days, contact, photo count and entered organization details. Telegram photos are not imported automatically. You preview the readable request or report, copy it or open the private @azamatdos chat, and send it yourself. Transferring text to external Telegram or the clipboard is a separate action, not local calculation; clipboard access may depend on OS rules, other apps and device synchronization. Opening Telegram does not confirm sending. The owner receives the text and materials you send, plus Telegram profile information available to the recipient: your name, username, photo and, if allowed by Telegram privacy settings, phone number. These are used to respond, moderate, publish and handle reports; the app does not automatically read private chats or your profile. Reports include a post ID/link and reason; a single report does not automatically remove a post. Do not send identity documents, national IDs, medical documents, CVV or unnecessary bank information. After confirmation, Clear in the request form deletes the current request and saved draft, including texts for deselected languages; original phone photos, protected order records and owner work are unaffected. Opening Telegram does not itself clear the draft.

What becomes public and when display stops

After approval, the owner publishes agreed texts, selected photos, contact and announcement details in a public Telegram channel. Others can view, copy and forward them. Private correspondence and profiles are not published automatically. Every placement requires timezone-aware start and end; UTC is used internally. Waiting for review does not consume the term; a day is exactly 24 hours. Display is allowed only while START <= now < EXPIRES. After expiry, the card, open screen, photos and contacts are hidden, including offline. This does not delete the original Telegram post or others’ copies. Expired records are excluded from persisted cache on the next load. Known revocations work offline; a new revocation must be received from the source. Monetary collections are hidden without confirmed freshness of the specific post of less than 5 minutes, including offline. Older charity posts without a help type are not treated as verified foundations.

Support, commercial placement and charity

Prayer times, Qibla, reminders, reading announcements and submitting a request are free. Voluntary support for the MyNamaz developer provides no benefits. Commercial placement is a separate paid service with moderation and a finite term; developer support does not pay for placement. The user chooses the amount; no fixed amounts are suggested. The support button offers external PayPal https://paypal.me/azamatdos or a private contact with @azamatdos to ask about another method. Opening a link does not confirm payment or message delivery. PayPal independently processes payment and technical data. The developer as recipient may see the sender’s name, email, amount and currency, date, transaction identifier and transfer message; the available details depend on the transaction type and PayPal rules. These are details in the recipient’s external PayPal account, not automatic collection of banking data by the app. MyNamaz does not receive card numbers, CVV or PayPal passwords. In-app store charges are disabled. Commercial announcements are separately agreed with the owner; placement requires their confirmation of actual payment receipt. The editor stores a payment-review decision, not bank credentials or a bank statement. Free charity placement has a selected finite term; monetary collections are allowed only for independently verified foundations and lead to a verified official foundation collection page. These donations do not pass through the developer’s PayPal or card. Personalized payer tracking is not added. Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing integration has been prepared for store placement purchases. Charges through this integration remain disabled until store setup and verification are complete. When purchasing is available, the store receives the product identifier and a random order identifier that the app binds to a specific request and duration; the request text and contact are not added to the payment request. The store, not MyNamaz, processes banking data. The booking approval, request, status, transaction identifier and signed purchase evidence are stored separately on the device: iOS ThisDeviceOnly Keychain or encrypted Android storage excluded from backup. They support reconciliation of unfinished purchases, duplicate-charge prevention and refunds; ordinary cache cleanup does not erase them. Privacy includes a separate Delete purchase data action. After confirmation it removes requests, contacts, approvals, transaction identifiers and evidence from the protected ledger. Erasure is blocked for an unfinished payment, uncertain outcome, ongoing placement or required refund: first contact @azamatdos with the order ID and finish reconciliation/refund. After erasure, minimal technical replay-protection records remain: a random salt and hashes of erased order and transaction identifiers. These are pseudonymous technical data, not requests, contacts or purchase evidence. They remain in protected device storage until that storage is erased; ordinary cache cleanup does not remove them. Erased requests are not automatically restored from store history. Erasure does not impose a permanent ban on new purchases: a new order requires a new approval and a current store check bound to that request, product and duration. This check is not connected yet, so real in-app charges remain disabled. A historical signature or editable flag does not prove current status, completed placement or a refund. Free features, external voluntary PayPal support and owner access are preserved. The developer cannot remotely erase your phone’s local storage. Ordinary app cleanup does not erase store history. Local deletion does not erase store history; iOS Keychain may survive app deletion. Signed owner approval authorizes a booking purchase but does not prove receipt of money. Copying a booking into a private chat is an explicit action; payment evidence is not included in public posts.

Access and sharing data

MyNamaz is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not intended for children. Google Play has been configured for an adult-only audience with a requested restriction on search, installation and purchases for accounts Google identifies as minors. Application of this restriction depends on Google Play review and rules; it does not automatically block previously installed or sideloaded copies or guarantee the age of every user. MyNamaz itself does not verify age or request a birth date. Do not submit children’s personal data through the app. If the developer learns of unnecessary child data, contact @azamatdos to request review and deletion of the developer’s copies under the procedure below. Prayer, Qibla, reminders, reading announcements, submitting requests and feedback are available without registration, a birth date or a separate private PIN. The app does not determine the user's age. External services, location, geocoding and maps process data as described above; these are not local-only operations. Before opening Telegram to submit a request, the app previews the selected language texts and contact. The user sends the message in Telegram; opening the chat does not confirm delivery. Photos sent separately must be attached by the user. Published contacts and materials become public. Reports also show their text before sending. Contact links and PayPal open when you choose them. Do not send strangers documents, your address or payment details. The separate six-digit PIN for ordinary actions has been removed. On startup the app erases the old local record: salt, derived PIN value, failure count, lockout time and any remaining markers of the previous setting. If protected storage is unavailable, cleanup retries when returning to the app or on the next launch. No data is sent to the developer. This cleanup does not affect owner permission or workspace, settings, drafts or purchase records. The owner editor still requires a separate permission and system authentication; biometric data stays with the OS. The store client does not start automatically when the app opens; real in-app charges and production payment-SDK startup remain disabled until setup and verification are complete. Entering panoramic Qibla shows a warning about nearby physical hazards and adult supervision for children.

Protected owner data

The owner editor locally stores requests, selected original photos, content and payment decisions, foundation reviews, post links, journal and organizer blocks. Up to 20 prior drafts and 200 brief journal entries remain until owner deletion or replacement by newer entries. A human performs review; no AI, translation service or bot is mandatory. Access requires a separate signed permission bound to the device key, then system authentication; biometrics alone confer no role. The app receives an authentication result, not a face image, biometric template or device passcode. iOS uses device-bound Keychain and file protection; Android uses AndroidKeyStore, encrypted workspace data and noBackupFilesDir. Photos are OS-protected private files. Backgrounding locks the editor; returning requires authentication. This grants no Telegram channel rights. Photos and text transfer externally only on the owner’s action. Required journal details are limited to the announcement, organization, official source, date, decision and brief reason. Selected original photos may contain EXIF, including capture location; the app does not promise automatic removal of this metadata before sharing.

Local retention and cleanup

Settings and consent remain until changed or app-data clearing; location cache remains until replacement or clearing. The official settlement list refreshes after 30 days and schedule cache after 7 days; these are refresh periods, not automatic deletion deadlines, and stale data may remain for offline use. A request draft remains until replaced or deleted by the user. “Clear local draft and cache” on the policy screen removes the draft, saved coordinates/names, schedule cache and announcement contents; settings, reminders, hidden IDs and revocation records remain. Temporary memory may hold the current screen until the app closes; new requests may refill caches. Hidden IDs preserve your choice; known revocations prevent withdrawn posts from reappearing. The editor can delete drafts and photos, or separately erase the whole workspace, journal and blocks while retaining owner permission. The editor does not replace mandatory accounting or tax records. Payment accounting information outside the app is not destroyed contrary to a mandatory applicable legal retention period or unresolved dispute; a deletion response explains the retention basis and ending event. PayPal and Telegram set their own retention rules. Accounting documents needed to substantiate taxable transactions in Kazakhstan are retained under Tax Code Article 206(3): at least five years starting with the following tax period and until the applicable limitation period ends if longer. This is not the retention period for every draft. Only necessary transaction details remain; unnecessary copies are deleted after the obligation and related disputes end. The reminder calculation location remains until rescheduling or app-data deletion and is not removed by this cache cleanup.

Backups and uninstalling

MyNamaz has no developer-operated cloud backup. Android app backup is disabled in this version; owner data lives in noBackupFilesDir. On iOS, private photo and activation folders are excluded from backup, and Keychain records do not migrate to another device; ordinary settings and caches may enter system backups according to Apple settings. Uninstalling removes the container, but iOS Keychain may retain the owner workspace, permission and device key after reinstalling. Delete unwanted workspace data through the editor before uninstalling; cleanup retains the owner role. Android uninstall normally removes app data and its Keystore keys. Copies you separately create in Telegram, Photos, system backups or the auxiliary desktop editor must be deleted there separately; this policy does not automatically erase old backups.

Request correction, deletion or withdrawal

Contact @azamatdos from your Telegram chat, stating what to delete or correct and the post ID/link or contact date. Do not send identity documents. The owner checks your connection to the material through the original chat, reviews necessary restrictions, deletes unnecessary local materials, prepares withdrawal/correction and edits or deletes their original Telegram post, then checks the source and reports the result and remaining copies/obligations. Contacts and nonpublic material are needed until the response, moderation, placement and related requests/disputes finish; unnecessary material must then be deleted by the owner. This is a manual procedure, not automatic Telegram cleanup. A minimal organizer-block record may remain while a justified decision against repeat fraud is in force; you can request review. Where law requires retention, the response explains exactly what remains and why. Deletion from all users, backups and independent services is not guaranteed. For your own Telegram/PayPal data you may also contact those services directly. The developer cannot access a reader’s local data; clear it on your device.

Public page and external policies

The policy page is public without registration and has no analytics, advertising or data-collection forms added by us. GitHub Pages (GitHub) hosting receives the IP address and technical request information for page delivery and security. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses even without account sign-in; retention follows its own rules. This site publishes only the policy, without application materials or app source code. External services may process data outside your country. Their policies: Telegram — https://telegram.org/privacy; PayPal — https://www.paypal.com/kz/legalhub/paypal/privacy-full; OpenStreetMap Foundation — https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy; Apple — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/; Google — https://policies.google.com/privacy; GitHub hosting — https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. New features and sources may require policy updates; the current version date appears here and in the app.
